# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to explore sales trends, customer demographics, order status, sales channels, and geographical performance.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales performance and order trends.
- Understand customer distribution by gender and age group.
- Identify top-performing states and sales channels.
- Examine order status distribution.
- Present key findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive slicers
- Excel formulas
- Data visualization and dashboard design

## Dashboard Features

- **Monthly Sales Analysis:** Explore sales and order trends across the year.
- **Gender Analysis:** Compare sales contributions by gender.
- **Age Group Analysis:** Examine sales distribution across customer age groups.
- **State-wise Analysis:** Identify high-performing states.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Compare performance across platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Analyze delivered, cancelled, refunded, and returned orders.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Use slicers to explore data by month, channel, and category.

## Key Insights

The analysis explores customer segments, geographical sales performance, monthly trends, and channel-wise contributions to identify potential business opportunities.

Specific findings and recommendations are presented in the accompanying Excel dashboard and insights worksheet.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on customer segments that contribute significantly to sales.
- Explore opportunities in lower-performing geographical markets.
- Evaluate sales channel performance when planning promotional campaigns.
- Use customer demographics to inform targeted marketing strategies.
- Investigate cancelled, returned, and refunded orders to identify potential improvements.
